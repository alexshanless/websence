# Google Business Profile assets

Not part of the site build. These are for upload to the GBP dashboard.

## Logo (pick one)

Google crops profile logos to a **circle** in several surfaces, so every mark
here keeps its ink inside a safe circle and is optically centred on the ink,
not on the text box.

- `logo-monogram-dark.png` / `logo-monogram-light.png` — 720x720.
  Recommended. Survives the circular crop and stays legible at the ~32px it
  renders at next to review replies.
- `logo-stacked-dark.png` / `logo-stacked-light.png` — 720x720.
  Full wordmark. Readable in square contexts, tight in a circle, and small at
  review-reply size.

Dark versions are the site's `#1a202c`. Light versions are for surfaces that
sit the logo on a dark ground.

## Cover

- `cover-1024x576.jpg` — Google's minimum cover size.
- `cover-1440x810.jpg` — same layout, more resolution. Prefer this one.

Both keep all content inside a centred safe area, because GBP crops the cover
differently on search, Maps, and mobile.
